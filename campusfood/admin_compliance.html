📋 Compliance Guidelines

Vendors should declare allergens clearly, including peanuts, tree nuts, milk/dairy, eggs, soy, fish, shellfish, and gluten. Dietary information such as Halal, Vegan, Vegetarian, and Gluten-Free is also recommended for student safety.
